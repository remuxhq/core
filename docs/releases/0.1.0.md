remux 0.1.0, the first release: the CLI and the engine, free, no account.

- macOS on Apple silicon (15+, OBS 32) and Linux on X11 (Ubuntu 24.04+, OBS 30+ from the distribution).
- `install.sh` says what it will do and asks; `remux daemon` runs the engine as a service of your session; `uninstall.sh` takes it all out.
- A relay and a chat of your own: `install-relay.sh` (mediamtx, one ffmpeg per platform), `install-chat.sh` (Twitch IRC and YouTube chat on the engine's wire).
- `remux bug` writes the report an issue needs, keys redacted; `--open` fills GitHub's form.
- Not yet: Wayland, Intel Macs, Windows.
