remux 0.3.0-rc.1, a pre-release of 0.3.0, for trying it before it is the one everybody gets.

- `remux sources` lists the screens, windows, applications, cameras and microphones; `remux devices` is gone, since windows and applications are not devices.
- `remux events -f` follows what changes as it happens, the chat's subs, gifts, raids, follows and bans included, and `remux chat say` sends a line to the chat.
- Image layers: `remux scene layer add image <id> <file>` puts a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP in the scene, moved, cropped and filtered like any layer.
- The gate no longer clicks when it opens, and the denoiser always runs before it, so the gate hears your voice and not the room.
- Everything under your voice ducks, an application's or a screen's sound included; `remux audio layer duck <id> on|off|auto` decides per layer.
- A chat bridge of your own (`remux chat --url`) carries only the chat; without an account, `title`, `describe`, `announce`, `categories` and `disconnect` say they need `remux login`.
- Each release carries its own `install.sh`: `releases/download/v0.3.0-rc.1/install.sh` installs this one, `releases/latest/download/install.sh` the newest stable.
- For developers: `make setup` installs nextest, llvm-cov and the scanners.
